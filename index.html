Privacy Policy

Vice Code: Cheats for GTA VI

How your data and privacy are handled when using the Vice Code mobile application.

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. No Personal Data Collection by the Developer

The developer of Vice Code: Cheats for GTA VI does not collect, store, track, or sell any personal data. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or location to use the app.

App preferences—such as your favorite cheats, selected console (PS5 or Xbox), theme choice, and 6-hour ad-free reward timers—are stored strictly locally on your device using AsyncStorage and are never transmitted to the developer.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses trusted third-party services from Google to support ads and optional in-app purchases. Those services may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display optional rewarded video ads and frequency-capped interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse diagnostic/performance data, and ad interaction data to serve, personalize (subject to your consent choices), and measure advertisements. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage ad privacy preferences anytime in Settings under Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process the optional one-time “Remove All Ads” in-app purchase and restore purchases. All payment and billing information is handled directly by Google Play; the developer never receives or stores your payment details.

3. Remote Cheat Updates

To keep cheat codes up to date without requiring an app update, the app fetches a public, read-only JSON catalog (cheats.json) over HTTPS. No user identifiers or personal information are sent in this request.

4. Children’s Privacy

Vice Code: Cheats for GTA VI is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Unofficial Fan App Disclaimer

Vice Code is an independent, unofficial fan-made companion app for Story Mode cheat codes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony Interactive Entertainment, or Microsoft Corporation.
